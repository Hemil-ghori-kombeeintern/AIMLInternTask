Our company is an AI and software development company.

The company develops artificial intelligence, machine learning,
web applications, mobile applications, and data engineering solutions.

Employees receive 20 days of annual leave every year.
Employees should submit their leave requests at least three
working days before the requested leave date.

The company provides health insurance to full-time employees.
Employees can contact the HR department for insurance-related questions.

The standard working hours are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM,
Monday through Friday.

The company uses Python, JavaScript, React, Node.js,
PostgreSQL, Docker, and cloud technologies for software development.

The AI team works on machine learning, deep learning,
natural language processing, computer vision, and generative AI.
